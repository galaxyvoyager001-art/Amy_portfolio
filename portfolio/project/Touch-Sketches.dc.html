The Kingdom of Shapes
Each book is a story set in the Kingdom of Shapes. In the first, Jianjian the triangle crosses town to a New Year concert, and every scene hides a triangle to find by touch. In the second, Fangfang the square changes into other four-sided shapes. I drew every page before a single piece of felt was cut.
Rules I designed by
1Every shape is raised: felt strips, metal or stitched edges.
2Every scene faces a braille card with a raised-line icon of its shape.
3Something on each page spins, rings or lights up.
4Nothing loose: thread ends are covered and small parts are sewn or sealed.
