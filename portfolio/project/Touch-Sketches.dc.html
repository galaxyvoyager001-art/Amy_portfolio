The Kingdom of Shapes
Our books follow Jianjian, a triangle who lives in Triangle Town. An invitation to the New Year concert sends her across the Kingdom of Shapes, and every scene on the way hides a triangle to find by touch: an obtuse-angled street, a cone-shaped gown, a traffic jam in a right angle, a Christmas tree, an equilateral conductor.
Rules I designed by
1Every shape is raised: felt strips, metal or stitched edges.
2Every scene faces a braille card with a raised-line icon of its shape.
3Something on each page spins, rings or lights up.
4Nothing loose: thread ends are covered and small parts are sewn or sealed.
