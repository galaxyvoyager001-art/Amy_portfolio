Sketchbook · The Kingdom of Shapes
Design drawings for the tactile books
Each book is a story set in the Kingdom of Shapes: Jianjian the triangle crosses town to a New Year concert, and Fangfang the square grows into a family of shapes. Before any felt was cut, I drew every spread in pencil: the layout, the loose parts, the joints in section and a cutting list, so the page could be built exactly and safely for small hands.
Four rules I designed by
Raisedevery shape in felt strips, metal or stitched edges
Pairedevery scene faces a braille card
Alivesomething spins, rings or lights up
Safeno loose ends, every small part sewn
