HOW A PAGE
IS MADE
One kit, five steps, thirty volunteers: how do pages made by different hands still match?
Every club member received a kit like this one: felt sheets, coloured felt strips, a braille story card and thread. With the same kit and the same five steps, thirty volunteers could sew pages that matched.
Fig. 1 · The kit for one page

The bench

The books were cut and sewn here, at the same bench where I build robots and science projects: a pegboard of tools, a 3D printer and a laptop for the design files.

Why a kit

A fixed kit meant every member worked with the same felt, strips, thread and card, so pages sewn by different people matched and the books felt consistent under a child's fingers.
