Curawave: A Wave-Bionic Soft Robotic Mattress for Post-Operative Patient Transfer
Design question. Can a mattress move a patient the way a wave moves a boat, safely and without anyone lifting?
Amy Hu, CTO · with Emily Feng (CEO) and Galatea Shang (CFO)
Summary  Among 1,200 post-operative patients in a study we cited, 15% experienced complications related to being transferred, and different moving devices and transition times influence secondary injury. Curawave is a mattress that imitates the movement of ocean waves to transfer patients safely and comfortably without human labour. It is built from 18 × 20 small soft units. Each unit has a hollow silicone inflation layer, a non-stretchable cotton confining layer and a silicone base; because the confining layer stretches less than the inflation layer, the unit deflects to one side when inflated. Adjacent units are grouped and inflated alternately, so a travelling wave carries the patient. We modelled the moulds in Autodesk Fusion, 3D-printed them, cast the silicone units, and drove an air pump through solenoid valves controlled by an Arduino, producing a working 1 × 4 prototype.

1 · Motivation

The idea began with my own bicycle accident: with an injured leg I struggled to move from place to place, and every attempt to move was shocked by pain. Moving patients after surgery is the same problem at hospital scale. In 2024 China had about 70 million people bedridden by injury and 96.4 million surgical operations.

2 · Design

Each unit stacks a waterproof, bacteria-resistant sheet, a cotton confining layer, a hollow silicone inflation layer and a silicone base. Inflating the unit bends it toward the stiffer side, so it pushes whatever lies on top.

Grouped units act alternately, so the mattress transfers patients automatically, can massage patients who cannot move, and is easy to clean.

3 · Making

① Moulds modelled in Autodesk Fusion: two bases and an inner core. ② Moulds 3D-printed and one unit cast and tested with silicones of different thickness. ③ Solenoid valves and an Arduino program control the inlet and outlet of the air pump. ④ A 1 × 4 model: the main pump's air is split four ways, each group switched by its own valve. ⑤ Further testing of the models.

4 · Market

China's medical-device market reached about 120 billion yuan, and surgery numbers keep rising. Our plan targets first-tier city hospitals first, with direct sales, training videos and annual maintenance; the five-year forecast grows turnover from ¥2.3M to ¥21.6M, with 1% given to charity each year.

5 · Next steps

Use AI to analyse the forces on a patient and adjust each unit's inflation; patent the way the units are assembled to mimic ocean waves; and extend beyond mainland China.
