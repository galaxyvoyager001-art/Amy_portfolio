A MATTRESS THAT
MOVES LIKE THE SEA
After a bicycle accident I could barely move from bed to chair without pain. Moving patients after surgery is the same problem at a larger scale: in a study we cited, 15% of 1,200 post-operative patients had complications linked to being transferred. Curawave is a mattress of 18 × 20 soft silicone cells. Each cell bends one way when inflated, because its cotton restriction layer stretches less than the silicone; groups inflate in turn, and the wave carries the patient across without anyone lifting.
