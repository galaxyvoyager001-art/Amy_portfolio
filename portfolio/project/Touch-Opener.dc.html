TOUCH
Even without sight,
there is still vision.
Our club runs one-to-one online tutoring for blind children: each volunteer is paired with one child for a weekly lesson, and there are now more than fifty pairs. This year we wanted to give the children something they could hold. Together we hand-sewed three tactile picture books in felt and braille, carried them to the children on a visit, filmed the day as a documentary, and closed the project with a club exhibition.
My roleOrganiser and book designer. I designed the books, planned the project and led the visit, and sewed pages with the team.
