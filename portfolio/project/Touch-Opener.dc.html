Even without sight,

Care & Reading, our school reading-tutor club of more than 100 members, works with ABF, a Beijing NGO for visually impaired children. Every week, each volunteer tutors one blind child online, and the same pairs stay together year after year. I led 30+ members to design and hand-sew tactile picture books in felt and braille, ran 10+ visits, made an exhibition and a short film, and raised over CNY 2,000.
My roleActivities leader & organiser. I designed the books, planned every visit and sewed pages with the team.
