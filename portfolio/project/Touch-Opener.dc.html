TOUCH
Even without sight,
there is still vision.
Can a picture book be read with the fingertips?
Care & Reading is our school's reading-tutor club of 100+ members. With ABF, a Beijing NGO for visually impaired children, we tutor blind children one-to-one every week, and I led 30+ members to design and hand-sew tactile picture books for them in felt and braille.
Fig. 1 · A finished book: every shape is raised, stitched or textured

The partner

ABF works with visually impaired children in Beijing. Most of the picture books children love depend on colour, so a blind child is often left with the words alone. We wanted books where the picture itself could be felt.

The pairs

Every week each volunteer tutors one child online, and the same pairs stay together year after year.

The books

Each book is a story from the Kingdom of Shapes. Every scene is raised in felt, metal or stitched edges and faces a braille story card, so a child can find the shape and read the line by touch.

My role

I designed the books, planned every visit, organised the sewing teams and sewed pages myself. I also ran the club exhibition and the short film, and raised over CNY 2,000 for materials.
