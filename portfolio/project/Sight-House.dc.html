SIGHT
A house in miniature
The tactile books asked how a story can be read by hand. This project asks the opposite question: how much can a world hold when it is read only by the eye? I shrank a whole home onto a desktop: five rooms on one floor, every piece made by hand.
01Plan the floorFive rooms on one level, with the bed lofted above the kitchen.
02Build the shellWalls, floor and loft must be square, or nothing inside sits straight.
03Make the furnitureBookcase, desk, bed and sofa, assembled with tweezers and glue.
04Dress every roomBedding, a towel, food on the plates: details that make it lived in.
Rooms5+
Build time10+ hrs
Hand-made parts200+
1 2 3 4 5 6
Making it small is not the hard part. The hard part is making every room believable.
The scale has to agree, the surfaces have to look used, and the light has to land the way it would in a real home.
