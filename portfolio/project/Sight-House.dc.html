A HOUSE IN
MINIATURE
A whole home, small enough to sit on a desk.
The tactile books asked how a story can be read by hand. This project asks the opposite question: how much can a world hold when it is read only by the eye? I shrank a whole home onto a desktop, with a study, a living room behind a round moon window, a loft bed, a kitchen and a dining table already set for dinner.
On scale"A teacup has to agree with the chair beside it, or the eye notices at once."
On precision"The smallest parts go in with tweezers. One extra drop of glue would show."
On atmosphere"Paper walls, fabric and a round moon window decide how each room feels."
