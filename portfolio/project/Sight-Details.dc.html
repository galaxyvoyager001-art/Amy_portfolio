02 — Sight · Look closerAmy Hu · 10
LOOK CLOSER
Every circle opens a room. Each one is a small world of its own.
The study
A desk and a full bookcase give the house someone's habits.
Tiny printed books, a desk lamp, brushes and a chair pulled up to work.
Dressing room
The small things of a morning routine.
A yellow towel on the rail, a basin, a red chair and slippers by the step.
Moon window
A round window frames the living room like a Chinese garden view.
Painted branches on the walls and a screen glowing with a mountain sunset.
Kitchen
Tucked under the loft bed, with every pot in its place.
Hanging utensils, a range hood and a row of drawers below.
Dining table
Dinner is already served for the family.
Plates of food, glasses and chopsticks on a patterned runner.
Tea table
The smallest set in the house sits in front of the sofa.
A teapot and cups smaller than a fingernail, shown on a book page for scale.
Making it small is not the hard part. The hard part is making every room believable: the scale has to agree, the surfaces have to look used, and the light has to land the way it would in a real home.
