The workbench, illustrated
Method
01
Plan the rooms
Sketch the floor plan and decide what each room is for before cutting anything.
02
Build the shell
Walls, floor and the round moon window go up first, so every later piece has a fixed reference.
03
Furnish
Bookcase, desk, sofa, loft bed and kitchen units are cut, glued and painted one by one.
04
Dress the rooms
Fabric, plants, books and a dinner of tiny dishes make the rooms feel lived in.
05
Light and photograph
Each room is lit from the side and shot on white so nothing distracts from the detail.
