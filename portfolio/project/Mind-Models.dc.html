IMMC 2024 · Panama Canal

Without water-saving basins, lock chambers transfer water from upstream to downstream, causing scarcity and drought risk. We derived how saved water depends on basin number and size, found the optimal basin scale, then combined energy, maintenance, water, labour and material costs into one model to find the cheapest way to build it.

IMMC 2025 · Robot jump rope

For a humanoid robot learning to jump rope, we treated the rope as a catenary and described the rotating shape with the Jacobi elliptic function sn, drawing it in 2-D and 3-D in MATLAB, and computed ranges for jump speed, jump time and rope length.

HiMCM 2025 · Sweeping a building

To plan responders' routes in an emergency, a cellular automaton models fire spread, smoke, occupant movement and room importance as potential fields; mixed-integer linear programming with CP-SAT then finds the route and team size that cover every cell in the shortest time, extended to restaurants, school labs and gas leaks.

IMMC 2026 · Friday the 13th

Disasters from 2000–2025 were scored for severity and compared with a "Disaster Severity Expectation Boltzmann Distribution"; information theory turned the gap into surprisal. Friday the 13th averaged 5.56 against a median of 4.42 and a maximum of 18.21, so no peak: the superstition is disproved. A graph model using Greedy Clique Expansion then predicted every date in 2025: all values lie within 7–8, so there is no obviously lucky or unlucky day.

IMMC 2026 · Etosha

A risk map, topological decomposition into patrol units, Snake-algorithm routes and a Kalman–Nyquist schedule decide where a small ranger team should patrol, and how often.

The risk model combines human-driven threats and natural environmental volatility into one metric, so limited rangers go where they are most needed. Within each unit, an Active Contour (Snake) algorithm draws a smooth closed patrol loop, refined by outward-bulging petals that reach residual high-risk clusters. The Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem sets a baseline patrol frequency, and the steady state of the Kalman filter gives a closed-form safety-critical revisit interval.
