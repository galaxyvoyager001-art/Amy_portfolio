For two years I led our school's first engineering and applied-mathematics journal: five editors, three issues, interviews with alumni and researchers, and more than 200 readers. On the other side of the page, I write the models myself. Five team papers for IMMC and HiMCM, three of them Finalist (F) awards, taught me to turn messy real problems into variables, assumptions and something we could test.
"Editing taught me to explain an idea. Modelling taught me to defend it."
