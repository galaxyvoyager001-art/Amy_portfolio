Section 05 · six research projects, 2023–2026
Research Portfolio:
Physics, Sound, Materials
My research asks the same question as the rest of this portfolio: how do people sense the world, and what can engineering do when a sense fails or a body is fragile? It runs from tournament physics to an AI hearing aid, from metallic glass under impact to a mattress that moves like the sea.
ContentsIn the order of my activity list
01
Energy Transport in Magnetically Coupled Pendulums
IB Physics Extended Essay · IYPT Macao team captain · CYPT 2026
How magnet strength, spacing and release angle shift the normal-mode frequencies of a magnetic Newton's cradle, measured on a rig I designed and 3D-printed.
p. 15
02
SmartHearing: A Multimodal Audio LLM-Based Personalized Intelligent Hearing Assistance System
Peking University, Institute for Artificial Intelligence · patent · first-author paper
A hearing aid that first understands the sound scene, then separates and boosts the sounds that matter, such as alarms a normal aid would treat as noise.
p. 16
03
Curawave: A Wave-Bionic Soft Robotic Mattress for Post-Operative Patient Transfer
Conrad Challenge · founder of Beijing Lingtong Future Technology
Soft silicone cells inflate in sequence so a travelling wave moves a patient across a bed without lifting. My startup raised over USD 100k.
p. 17
04
Flowed Metal: Micro-particle Impacts on Metallic Glasses under High Temperature
Chinese Academy of Sciences, Institute of Mechanics · ISEF Beijing qualifier
Laser-launched particles hit Pd-based metallic glass at up to 700 m/s; at 200 °C the glass flows and its dynamic yield strength drops by about 10%.
p. 18

Polarisation Control and Experimental Optics

Twelve undergraduate optics experiments written up as full lab reports, then Raman and laser systems and more than twenty polarisation states.

Mathematical Modelling and Science Publishing
