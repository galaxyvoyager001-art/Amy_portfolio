SMARTHEARING
A hearing aid that knows which sound matters right now.
Conventional hearing aids put speech first, so a siren, an alarm or a scream can be turned down as "noise". SmartHearing listens like a person does. A large audio-language model describes the scene in words; a language-queried separator pulls out the sounds those words name; and an adaptive window of 4 to 10 seconds keeps the delay low. A lightweight Query-space Adapter closes the gap between how the model describes sounds and how the separator was trained.
"My grandfather's hearing challenges taught me to notice quiet details. This project is about the loud ones that must never be missed."
