15MAGNETIC
NEWTON'S CRADLE
Repelling magnets that never touch, yet still pass energy along.
The key equation
L = ½ IeqΣ θ̇i² − Σ Mg(l+R)(1−cos θi) − Σ Vmag,ij
Ieq θ̈n + ∂V∂θn + γ θ̇n = 0
Lagrangian with Rayleigh damping · one equation per pendulum
What I found
1The motion is a mix of normal modes: the first frequency is set by gravity, the second by the magnets.
2Release angle sets the dominant frequency; spacing and magnet strength tune the second one.
3Model and experiment agree within ~3%; the chain is non- or only weakly chaotic.
02ELECTRICAL
DAMPING
A magnet on a spring, slowed down by a coil and a resistor.
The key equation
Fem = − G(x, N, B)²R · ẋ   ⇒   γem = G²R
G = mutual-inductance gradient between magnet and coil
What I found
1Damping rises with coil turns, peaks near 2,700 turns, then falls as the coil's own inductance takes over.
2The damping coefficient falls as 1/R: more resistance, less current, less braking.
3For weak magnets it grows with the square of the magnetic moment, then levels off.
09LEVITATION
CONTROL
Graphite floating over magnets, steered by a beam of light.
The key equation
Fr = h2μ0 · dχ∥dT · dTdr ∬ Bxy² dA0
Light → temperature gradient → susceptibility gradient → force
What I found
1The sheet floats where diamagnetic lift balances its weight.
2Light heats one corner; graphite's susceptibility drops with temperature, so the sheet is pushed away from the light.
3Simulation: a 1.36 K difference across the sheet, and a gradient that stays constant under steady light.
