Introduction
  • In a Newton's cradle, energy passes from ball to ball by collision.
  • Replace the balls with repelling magnets and energy still travels, but without contact, the way atoms in a crystal trade energy through forces.
  • A chain of magnetic pendulums is therefore a desktop model of a lattice: energy can travel along it or stay localised.

Research Question
  • How do magnet strength, spacing and release angle affect the normal-mode frequencies of a chain of magnetically coupled pendulums?
  • How do those frequencies decide whether energy is transported or localised?

Results and Discussion
Energy moving pendulum to pendulum
Time–frequency spectrograms of three pendulums in the chain
  • The motion is a mix of normal modes: the first frequency is set mainly by gravity, the second by the magnetic coupling.
  • The release angle decides which frequency dominates; spacing and magnet strength tune the second.
  • Model and experiment agree within about 3%.
  • The chain is non- or only weakly chaotic, so energy can be followed as it travels or stays localised.
Conclusion
  • Changing the coupling changes the modes, and the modes decide where the energy goes: a tabletop analogue of energy transfer in a crystal lattice.
