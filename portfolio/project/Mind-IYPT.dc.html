A cradle that
never touches.never touches.
In a Newton's cradle, energy passes by collision. Replace the balls with repelling magnets and it passes without contact, the way atoms in a crystal trade energy through forces. I asked how magnet strength, spacing and release angle shift the chain's normal-mode frequencies, and how that decides whether energy travels or stays trapped. I designed and 3D-printed the rig, tracked every trial on video, extracted eigenfrequencies by FFT and compared them with a Lagrangian model.

1The motion mixes normal modes: gravity sets the first frequency, the magnets set the second.

#2Electrical Damping
↓ the picture becomes an equation
Fem = − G(x, N, B)² ẋ / R
γem = G² / R
↓ what I found
1Damping rises with coil turns, peaks near 2,700 turns, then falls as the coil's inductance takes over.
2The damping coefficient falls as 1/R.
3For weak magnets it grows with the square of the magnetic moment, then levels off.
