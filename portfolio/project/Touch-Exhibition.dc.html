Exhibition · the club's closing show
EVEN WITHOUT SIGHT,
THERE IS STILL VISION
What happens when sighted visitors read with their hands?
We closed the project with a club exhibition. Classmates picked up the books and learned how blind people move through and perceive the world, a group most of them had never thought about.
What visitors wrote
"I like that you let visitors touch and interact instead of simply reading about blindness."— a teacher
"The tactile books are thoughtful, but you could explain more clearly how each texture helps a blind child understand the story."— a classmate
"Consider adding feedback from visually impaired users themselves to strengthen the exhibition."— a classmate
"It made me wonder what everyday objects would feel like if I could only understand them through touch."— a classmate
