THE BOOKS
Two of our three books, both set in the Kingdom of Shapes. Each spread pairs a felt scene with a braille story card, and the raised-line icon on the card tells the child which shape to look for next.

"My gown! My heels!" She slips on a cone-shaped gown and rushes out.
