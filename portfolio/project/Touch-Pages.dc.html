TheBOOKS
Two of our three books, both set in the Kingdom of Shapes. Each spread pairs a felt scene with a braille story card, and the raised-line icon on the card tells the child which shape to find next. Book 1 hides a triangle in every scene; in Book 2 one square turns into something new on every page.
BOOK 1 · JIANJIAN THE TRIANGLE8 spreads · triangles of every kind
01 · Triangle Town
"My name is Jianjian. I live in Triangle Town."
Craft sticks form loose triangles a child can lift and rearrange; the red roof is a raised felt triangle.
02 · The horn
"Toot toot! No. 3 Obtuse Street, a letter!"
A real toy horn, a compass and a coiled cord give the street its sounds and textures.
03 · The invitation
"My gown! My heels!" A cone-shaped gown.
A felt envelope with stitched edges opens onto a wooden cone; a metal clef hints at the concert.
04 · The gown
"Honk honk!" Cars in a right triangle.
A floral fabric skirt hangs on a real hanger between felt triangles, so the gown can be lifted and felt.
05 · Traffic jam
A Christmas tree glows at the hall door.
Craft-stick roads, toy cars and road signs, all sewn down so nothing comes loose.
06 · The tree
An equilateral conductor keeps equal beats.
Stacked felt triangles make the tree, decorated with buttons, a sock and a snowflake.
07 · The triangle
A waiter carries fizzy drinks.
A real metal triangle and beater: the child can strike it and hear the shape.
08 · Countdown
"10, 9, 8 … 1!" The New Year bell rings.
A cup, a ladder and stick fireworks on blue felt; the bell icon matches the New Year bell.
BOOK 2 · FANGFANG'S TRANSFORMATIONS8 spreads · one square, eight new shapes
01 · Fangfang
Meet Fangfang, a little square who loves to change shape.
A yarn-haired face with button eyes opens the book and introduces Fangfang.
02 · Flying carpet
She flies up into a parallelogram: whoosh, a flying carpet!
Bubbled fabric gives the carpet a bumpy surface to explore under the fingertips.
03 · Car
She crouches into a trapezoid: vroom, a car!
A felt racetrack, a traffic light and a toy fire engine that can be rolled along.
04 · Rattle drum
She gets cross and puffs up round: boom, a drum!
A wooden pellet drum wrapped in felt rattles when the handle is twisted.
05 · Tree
Into the forest, layer on layer: a big tree.
Square felt leaves with real buttons on a twig branch can be buttoned and unbuttoned.
06 · Flower
She spins into a hexagon: a flower!
A felt flower with a button centre stands in a glittered pot.
07 · School bag
Pieced together, neat and square: a school bag.
Real backpack buckles on a felt bag let children practise clipping them together.
08 · Gem
A somersault, and she is a rhombus: a gem!
A stretchy pop tube coiled into a ring can be pulled, bent and squeezed.
