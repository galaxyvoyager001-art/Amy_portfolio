What does a reading partner change for a blind child?
We have visited the children more than ten times. Each visit starts with ice-breaker games, then a workshop such as My Little Planet, where children paint with materials they can feel. We read the books together, interview the NGO teachers, and filmed one visit as a short documentary.
50+children learn beyond what their schools currently teach, one-to-one, every week.
1:1Consistent, not one-off: each child keeps the same volunteer week after week.
