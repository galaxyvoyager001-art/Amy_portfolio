Hearing the gramophone age again.
An acoustic-era disc sounds muffled because the horn that recorded it lost almost everything above 8 kHz. With my teammate, I repaired and built phonographs, measured exactly what they distort, and designed a way to undo it.
01
Repair & build
Restored an antique Victor soundbox and built a motor-driven turntable to play and record discs.
02
Denoise & EQ
Compared FIRWIN2 filtering with Dynamic Spectrum Matching (DSM); DSM improved sound quality by 34%.
03
Restore & donate
Averaged DSM into one gain curve that repairs discs with no original, restored 103 recordings from 1901–1925 and donated them back.
04
Archive & exhibit
Built Sound Time Map, an interactive archive of every disc, and shared it with 300+ visitors.
