THE PHYSICS
OF BAKING
Twelve months of recipes, science and self.
I wrote, baked, photographed and designed this 38-page book. Each month pairs one recipe with a page on the physics that makes it work: how whipped egg whites hold air, why folded butter turns into flaky layers, when a crust begins to brown.
