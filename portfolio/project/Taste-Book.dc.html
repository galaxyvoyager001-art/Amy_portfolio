What does a croissant have to do with physics?
I baked, photographed, wrote and designed all 38 pages myself. Each month pairs one recipe with a page on the physics that makes it work: how whipped egg whites hold air, why folded butter turns into flaky layers, when a crust begins to brown. The finished book is on sale on Amazon.
