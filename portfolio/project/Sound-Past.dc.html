HEARING
THE PAST
A memory space that older people build by talking.
Reminiscence sessions often start from old photos and objects, but many older people no longer have pictures from those years, and stock images of old Shanghai or old Beijing never match the lane they grew up in. So I reversed the process: instead of showing a ready-made picture, the system builds the place while the person describes it. They only have to talk.
01
A companion that asks
An AI companion greets them and follows their story, one question at a time: what did the doorway look like?
02
The street appears
As they speak, a 360° street view forms, and the tree, the bicycle and the wonton stall are placed into it.
03
Sound in its place
Bicycle bells, street calls and opera from an old radio come from the right direction, fainter with distance and muffled behind walls.
04
Corrections in plain words
"No, the wonton seller was at the other end." The stall moves; when unsure, the system asks first.
05
Stories pinned to places
When they talk about a person or an event, the recording is attached to that spot in the scene.
06
Easy to stop
"Too loud" silences everything within half a second; if they seem tired, the companion offers a rest.

Design choices

Voice only. No buttons and nothing to learn; the staff panel sits in a corner the participant does not see.

Rules for the companion. It never invents or corrects a memory, asks one question at a time, and speaks slowly and briefly, so the session never feels like a test.

Period texture. Photographic images rather than illustration, and sound shaped like an old radio, loudspeaker or gramophone.

Ethics & research

Participants are identified by number only, consent comes first, and data stays on the local machine and can be exported or deleted at any time.

Three built-in conditions test whether a co-built scene draws out more memories: sound only, a generic regional scene, and a scene built from the person's own account. Each session produces a report of speaking turns, stories told and scene edits, comparable across sessions.

How it is built

three.js for the 360° view and occluding objects; Web Audio for spatial sound, distance and period filters; Alibaba Cloud speech recognition and synthesis; Qwen to converse and turn speech into scene edits; Wanxiang to generate scenes and objects; depth estimation for occlusion; a Python FastAPI back end that versions every edit.

My role. Concept, interaction flow, the companion's rules, sound and visual style, and the research conditions. I built the full working system with AI coding tools.
