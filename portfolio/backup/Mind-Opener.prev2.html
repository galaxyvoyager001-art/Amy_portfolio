Section 05 · six research projects, 2023–2026
Research Portfolio: Physics, Acoustics, Materials and Assistive Engineering
My research asks the same question as the rest of this portfolio: how do people sense the world, and what can engineering do when a sense fails or a body is fragile? Each entry gives the paper's title, where the work was done, my role, and a summary drawn from its abstract.

01
Energy Transport and Localization in a Chain of Magnetically Coupled Pendulums
IB Physics Extended Essay · IYPT Macao team captain · CYPT 2026
A chain of magnetically coupled pendulums is a macroscopic analogue of systems in which interacting particles exchange and transport vibrational energy. A Lagrangian model built from independently measured parameters reproduced the measured normal-mode frequencies within about 1.4%.
p. 15
02
SmartHearing: Scene-Adaptive Hearing Assistance via LALM-Guided Language-Queried Sound Enhancement
Peking University, Institute for AI · patent · co-first-author paper (ICASSP 2027 submission)
Conventional hearing aids prioritize speech, yet which sound matters most (a siren, an alarm, a scream) depends on the acoustic scene. In a 101-participant listening test the system was rated most salient in 50.5% of trials.
p. 16
03
Curawave: A Wave-Bionic Soft Robotic Mattress for Post-Operative Patient Transfer
Conrad Challenge · founder of Beijing Lingtong Future Technology
Inflatable silicone cells bend one way and inflate in sequence, so a travelling wave carries a patient across a bed without lifting. My startup raised over USD 100k.
p. 17
04
Flowed Metal: Micro-particle Impacts on Metallic Glasses under High Temperature
Chinese Academy of Sciences, Institute of Mechanics · first author · ISEF Beijing qualifier
The impact response of a Pd₄₃Ni₁₀Cu₂₇P₂₀ bulk metallic glass at 25, 100 and 200 °C was investigated via laser-induced particle impact tests and finite-element simulations; a ~10.4% reduction in dynamic yield strength confirmed thermal softening.
p. 18

Twelve undergraduate optics experiments written up as full lab reports, from Newton's rings to Michelson interferometry, then Raman and laser systems and more than twenty polarisation states.
