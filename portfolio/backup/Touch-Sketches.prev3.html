FROM DRAWING
TO FELT
Design drawings for the Kingdom of Shapes, drawn before a single piece of felt was cut.
Each book is a story set in the Kingdom of Shapes: Jianjian the triangle crosses town to a New Year concert, and Fangfang the square grows into a family of shapes. Before any felt was cut, I drew every spread in pencil as a plan, with its parts laid out, joints in section and a cutting list, so the finished page could be built exactly and safely.
Design rules
R1Every shape is raised: felt strips, metal or stitched edges.
R2Every scene faces a braille card with a raised-line icon of its shape.
R3Something on each page spins, rings or lights up.
R4Nothing loose: thread ends are covered and small parts are sewn or sealed.
