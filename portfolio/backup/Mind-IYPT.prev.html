A CRADLE THAT
NEVER TOUCHES
How energy travels along a chain of magnetic pendulums.
In a Newton's cradle, energy passes by collision. Replace the balls with repelling magnets and it passes without contact, the way atoms in a crystal trade energy through forces. I asked how magnet strength, spacing and release angle shift the chain's normal-mode frequencies, and how that decides whether energy travels or stays trapped. I designed and 3D-printed the rig, tracked every trial on video, extracted eigenfrequencies by FFT and compared them with a Lagrangian model.
"A tournament question became my Extended Essay: a desktop analogue of energy transfer in a lattice."

#15 Magnetic Newton's Cradle
Key equation
L = ½ IeqΣθ̇i² − Σ Mg(l+R)(1−cos θi) − Σ Vmag,ij
Ieq θ̈n + ∂V∂θn + γ θ̇n = 0
What I found
1The motion mixes normal modes: the first frequency is set by gravity, the second by the magnets.
2Release angle sets the dominant frequency; spacing and magnet strength tune the second.
3Model and experiment agree within ~3%; the chain is non- or weakly chaotic.
#2 Electrical Damping
Key equation
Fem = − G(x, N, B)²R · ẋ  ⇒  γem = G²R
What I found
1Damping rises with coil turns, peaks near 2,700 turns, then falls as the coil's own inductance takes over.
2The damping coefficient falls as 1/R.
3For weak magnets it grows with the square of the magnetic moment, then levels off.
