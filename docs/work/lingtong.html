I

For whom

A paper proves an idea works; only a product reaches the person who needs it. Lingtong exists for the users of both projects: people losing their hearing, and patients who must be moved.

SmartHearing+Curawave→Lingtong
II

The problem

Research ends with a prototype on a lab bench. Between that and a device in someone’s home there is design, certification, manufacturing, sales and service, and none of it happens without a company.

III

What we built

For the first 18 months, 70–80% of our resources go to SmartHearing; Curawave continues as the second growth curve.

SmartHearingAI hearing system that decides which sound you need to hear. Patent filed.
CurawaveWave-bionic transfer mattress for hospitals and care homes. Three prototype generations.

Three ways to earn: own-brand devices with long-term service (basic hearing never behind a subscription), institutional solutions for hearing centres, care homes and hospitals, and licensing an Auditory Decision Engine SDK to makers of earbuds, hearing aids and smart glasses.
